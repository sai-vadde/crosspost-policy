How to delete your data

If my tool, Hrushikesh Replies, has anything about you (your Instagram ID and username, your comment, your reply), you can ask me to delete it.

Ask me

  1. Send a direct message to @hrushikesh_vadde saying "delete my data", or email hrushikeshvadde@gmail.com from the address you want me to reply to.
  2. I delete everything linked to your Instagram account within 30 days, and I tell you when it's done.

Or cut the connection yourself

In Instagram: Settings and activity, then Apps and websites, and remove the app. This stops it from reaching my account. It doesn't delete what I already hold, so send me the message above as well.

More about what I keep is in the Privacy Policy.
