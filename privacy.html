Privacy Policy

I'm Sai Hrushikesh Vadde (@hrushikesh_vadde on Instagram). I made a small tool, Hrushikesh Replies, that answers comments on my own Instagram reels with a private message. It is for my account only. Nobody else uses it.

What it looks at

  • Your comment on my reel (the text and when you wrote it).
  • Your Instagram ID and username, as Instagram gives them to me.
  • If you reply to my message: your reply.
  • Sometimes, whether you follow my account. I only use this to choose the wording of my message. Following is always optional, and you never have to follow to receive anything.

What it does with it

It sends you a small number of private messages in reply (typically 1 to 3), with the link I promised, and it may post a short public reply to your comment. It makes sure you don't get the same message twice, and it never sends bulk or unsolicited messages. That's all. I don't use it for ads, profiling or analytics, and I don't sell or share it.

Where it lives, and for how long

On a computer I control, in a private database that only I can open. I delete it within 90 days after a campaign ends, and sooner if you ask.

Who else sees it

Instagram and Meta, because the tool talks to Instagram through its official API. Their own privacy policy covers what they do. No one else.

Your choices

You can ask me to show you or delete what I have about you. See how to ask for deletion. You can also stop the tool from reaching my account any time in Instagram, under Settings and activity, Apps and websites.

The tool isn't meant for children under 13, and I don't knowingly keep anything about them.
